## Course-wide changes

- Reframed the materials for an algebra- and trigonometry-based College Physics course serving primarily non-physics and non-engineering majors.

- Removed required Python, Colab, NumPy, Matplotlib, and computational-verification expectations.
- Replaced the programming primer with a Markdown and LaTeX writing reference.
- Converted the Matplotlib figures notebook into a no-code figures and graph-literacy primer.
- Updated worked-solution expectations to use Problem, Model, Math, and Conclusion.
- Replaced potentially stale grading percentages with a reference to the current syllabus and gradebook.
- Preserved the physics pathways material while labeling it as optional exploration for students who may consider a physics major, minor, or related path.
- Clarified whenever pathway graphics refer to computation that those skills belong to the broader degree or later career preparation, not PHYS 1401 assignments.
